Privacy Policy for Una Cosa

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

Una Cosa is a productivity application designed to help users focus on a single important task and improve personal organization. It has been developed following a privacy-by-default approach: it operates entirely on the device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal user data.

Developer: Applicados
Contact: infogooweb@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

  • The application does not require user registration.
  • We do not request or store names, email addresses, or passwords.
  • Tasks, settings, and preferences are stored only on the user's device.
  • We do not collect usage statistics or analytics data.
  • We do not transmit information to external servers.

3. Local Storage

All information managed by Una Cosa is stored locally on the user's device using the application's private storage mechanisms.

  • Data is not sent to external servers.
  • Data is not synchronized with cloud services.
  • Data remains under the exclusive control of the user.

4. Notification Permission

Una Cosa may request permission to display local notifications related to task reminders or alerts configured by the user.

  • Notifications are generated exclusively on the device.
  • We do not use external services to deliver notifications.
  • Notifications can be disabled from the application or the device settings.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or personal documents.
  • We do not use tracking, telemetry, or monitoring tools.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not sell user information.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Una Cosa does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising networks.

The application also does not use analytics services, user tracking, advertising profiling, or remote usage statistics collection.

7. Data Export and Backups

If the application includes export or backup features, they are performed only at the user's request.

  • Exported files are stored only in the location selected by the user.
  • The protection of exported files outside the application is the user's responsibility.
  • The application does not retain external copies of exported files.

8. Children

Una Cosa is intended for the general public and does not collect personal information from children or any other users.
